# Everlearn Profiles

Profiles tune examples and folder suggestions. They do not change the core capture workflow.

## General

Best for personal knowledge, everyday work, and broad learning.

Common captures:

- lessons from a project
- decisions and tradeoffs
- useful research summaries
- repeatable workflows
- prompts worth reusing

## Ecommerce

Best for sellers, operators, marketplace teams, and growth work.

Common captures:

- customer objections and buying friction
- product review patterns
- ad and keyword learnings
- competitor positioning
- bundle and pricing ideas
- marketplace policy notes

Suggested tags:

`ecommerce`, `amazon`, `customer-signal`, `ads`, `listing`, `pricing`, `product-research`

## Coding

Best for engineering work.

Common captures:

- repo-specific patterns
- architecture decisions
- debugging paths
- verification commands
- deployment runbooks
- reusable prompts for code review or implementation

Suggested tags:

`coding`, `repo-learning`, `architecture`, `debugging`, `verification`

## Research

Best for source-heavy synthesis.

Common captures:

- claims and evidence
- source notes
- open questions
- contradictions
- synthesis memos

Suggested tags:

`research`, `source-note`, `evidence`, `synthesis`

## Operations

Best for process and business operations.

Common captures:

- SOPs
- recurring review notes
- team decisions
- handoff learnings
- process bottlenecks

Suggested tags:

`operations`, `sop`, `handoff`, `process`, `review`
