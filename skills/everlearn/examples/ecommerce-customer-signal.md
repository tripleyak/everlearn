# Customer asked for a cheaper bundle

## Capture

A shopper wanted a lower-priced starter bundle before buying the full kit.

## Why It Matters

- This could indicate a price ladder gap rather than weak demand.

## Reuse

- Check review language, search terms, and competitor bundles for similar starter-kit demand.
- Test a lower-priced bundle before changing the main product price.
