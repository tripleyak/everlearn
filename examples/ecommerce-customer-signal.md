# Customer asked for a cheaper bundle

## Capture

A shopper wanted a lower-priced starter bundle before buying the full kit.

## Why It Matters

- This could point to a price ladder gap instead of weak demand.

## Reuse

- Compare review language, search terms, and competitor bundles.
- Test a starter bundle before discounting the core product.
